# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in VoiceFlow welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the repository maintainers through the security
contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept
confidential where possible.
